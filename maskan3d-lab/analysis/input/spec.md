# Product Spec: Maskan 3D (copied from project document, draft 1, 1 Nov 2026)
Note from the document: all numbers are proposed and adjustable.

Reference look: Matterport-style dollhouse of a multi-room apartment: tilted top view, clean closed walls, complete floors with no gaps, real colours/materials from the photos, visible furniture, ceiling cut away to show the interior.
Required accuracy: good enough for marketing (correct sense of space and proportions), not 100% engineering-grade measurement.
Viewer: interactive, KIRI-Engine-like: one-finger rotate, two-finger zoom, pan, ceiling cut.

| # | Criterion | Minimum acceptable | Ambition |
|---|-----------|--------------------|----------|
| 1 | Dollhouse look | No white holes, real colours from the photos | Straight closed walls, clean furniture, close to Matterport |
| 2 | Dimensions accuracy | Close to reality (error up to 10 cm in wall length) | Error 5 cm or less |
| 3 | Interactive viewer | Smooth rotate / zoom / pan by touch | Ceiling cut and fast focus on any room |
| 4 | Mobile performance (mid-range phone) | At least 30 FPS | 50 FPS or more |
| 5 | File size for viewing | 25 MB or less | 15 MB or less |
| 6 | Processing time per apartment | Less than 1 hour | Less than 20 minutes |
| 7 | Capture input | 80 to 200 photos or a walking video | Fewer captures with same quality |
| 8 | Equipment | Ordinary Android phone only | No extra devices |
| 9 | Cost | Free or cheap to run (Colab T4 at the start) | Cheap, scalable infrastructure |
| 10 | Licences | Open-source components with commercially safe licences | No non-commercial component at all |
| 11 | UI | Arabic, right-to-left | (none) |
